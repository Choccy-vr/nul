# nul
A WLED controller that blends into the background

<img width="600" height="800" alt="Flyer" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c70838f5-079c-446c-929f-8f16712e1e62" />

## Description
Nul is a small, compact WLED controller that is meant to go in line with your LED strip. Powered by an ESP32 C3 in a super small 11x40mm package.

## Instructions 
1. Plug the board into your computer and update WLED if you need to (preinstalled with v16.0.1)
2. Connect your LED Strip to your board
3. Attach your antenna
4. Plug in any USB PD Power source and enjoy
5. Connect to the WLED-AP Access point and connect it to the internet.
